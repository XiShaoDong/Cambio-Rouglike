Cambio is a fast paced card game that tests your awareness, strategy, and reaction time
  • Play on any device. Mobile and desktop friendly!
  • Play with 2 or more friends (3-6 is ideal)
  • Most similar to Cabo the card game (see rules for differences)

Win by having fewest points in your hand
Points are shown at the bottom right of each card:

  • A = 1
  • 2 = 2
  • J = 11
  • Q = 12
  • Red K = -1
  • Black K = 13
  • There are also two jokers in the deck worth 0 points

You start with four cards, with two cards visible only to you. Memorize this as you cannot see the cards again once the game starts!

On your turn, draw from the deck or discard pile.

After drawing, you can either discard it to use the card's power, or replace one of your own cards with it .

If you drew from the discard pile, you cannot use that card's power.

Card Powers

  • 7, 8 - Peek at one of your own cards
  • 9, 10 - Peek at someone else's card
  • J, Q - Swap any two cards without looking
  • K - Choose two cards to look at and decide to swap them

No need to memorize the powers. The game will let you know when the time comes

Flipping over cards
At any point you can tap someone else or your own card to flip it over.

If that card matches the top card in the discard pile, you flipped correctly! If you flipped your own card, you now have one fewer card (which is good!). If you flipped someone else's card, you can choose a card from your hand to give them.

If you flipped incorrectly, you will gain a card. If you have more than 6 cards, you lose.

Only one person can flip successfully per discard, and you cannot flip on your own discard from your turn.

Winning
Once you feel you have the fewest points out of everyone, on your turn before you draw a card, you can press "call cambio" and immediately end your turn.

Everyone else has one more turn, and after that the game ends.

After you called cambio, no one can touch your cards, and you cannot flip additional cards.

If you indeed have the fewest points, you win! If someone else ties you or has fewer points, they win!
